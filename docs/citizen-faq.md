# Citizen FAQ

**Do I need an account?** No. Open the portal, tap **Report a road problem**, take a photo and send it.

**What should the photo show?** The damage itself, from a few steps away, in daylight if possible. One problem per report.

**My location was not found.** Allow location for the site or app, or tap the map to drop a pin on the damage.
If neither works, we use the location stored in the photo, if it has one.

**Is my identity public?** No. Faces and number plates are blurred before the photo is stored or checked. Your note
and email are only for the ward office. The portal never shows anything that identifies you.

**What happens after I send it?** The AI inspector names the problem and scores it from 1 to 10. That sets the repair
deadline: 24 hours for critical, 48 hours for high, 7 days for medium. The ward office assigns a crew, and every step
appears on the report page.

**The app said "a ward engineer will check it".** The AI was unsure or busy. A person will look at the photo; it
becomes public once confirmed.

**"Already reported by others"?** Someone reported the same spot. Your report was added to theirs, which moves it up
the queue. You can also tap **I see this too** on any open report.

**How do I follow my report?** Use the tracking link shown after sending, or **My reports** on the same phone.
Reports that changed since your last visit are highlighted. If you gave an email, you also get a message when it
is assigned and when it is fixed.

**No internet?** Send anyway. The report waits on your phone and goes out automatically when you are back online.

**How do I know it was really fixed?** Resolved reports show the crew's after photo next to yours, and the AI checks
that the damage is gone. If it still sees damage, the report is reopened.

**Is the data open?** Yes. Anyone can download all published reports as CSV or use the JSON API (see "Open data and API").
