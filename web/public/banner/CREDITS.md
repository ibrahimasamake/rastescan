# Banner photos: sources and rights

These photos are the news shots for the Home banner reel, which plays 4 shots in all: the first two in `meta.json`, each followed by a live report. They are news photographs of Indian potholes. **The rights belong to
the publishers, not to this project.** They are used here, credited on screen, for a student project demonstration only.
Get permission from the publisher, or replace them with your own photos, before any wider public or commercial use.
