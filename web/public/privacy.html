RasteScan privacy policy

RasteScan is a student project shown at the TechKubik National Level Project Expo 2026, HKBK College of Engineering, Bengaluru.

What we collect

  • The road photo you send. Faces and number plates are blurred automatically before the photo is stored.
  • Your location when you send a report, so the ward office knows where the damage is.
  • A random device code stored in the app, so you can see your own reports. We do not collect your name, phone number or email.

How we use it

Photos are analysed by an AI model on Amazon Web Services to classify the damage and rate its severity. Reports are shown to ward engineers on the RasteScan dashboard. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Reports and photos are kept for the duration of the project demonstration and deleted afterwards.
